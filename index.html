PolyQuest

Learn everything. A free iPhone app where an AI model of your choice builds courses and lessons on any topic.

PolyQuest is free, has no ads and no account. You connect your own AI provider (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or a custom endpoint) with your own API key, and pay the provider directly for what you use.

Made by one person. If you like it, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi. It never unlocks anything.
