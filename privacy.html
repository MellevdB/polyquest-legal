Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short

PolyQuest has no server and no account. We do not collect, store or sell your data. Everything you create stays on your iPhone, and the only place your learning requests go is the AI provider you choose yourself.

What stays on your device

Your name, location and interests (if you enter them), your courses, lessons, progress and settings are stored on your iPhone. Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only.

What is sent to your AI provider

To create courses and lessons, PolyQuest sends your requests (for example the topic you want to learn, your level, and optionally personal details such as your interests that you chose to share) directly from your iPhone to the AI provider you selected: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or a custom endpoint you entered. That provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy, and bills you for usage through your own API account. We never see these requests or the answers.

Chat, explanations and your theme

When you chat with Poly, ask for an explanation or report a question, the text of that request (and the recent conversation) is sent directly to your chosen AI provider, like course requests. To design your personal theme, PolyQuest sends your chosen interests, place, mood and favourite colour once; your name is never sent. Conversations are kept on your iPhone.

Your own material

You can build a course from your own notes, a book or photos of pages. Files and photos are read on your iPhone (text extraction and on-device text recognition). Only the extracted text is sent to your chosen AI provider, in the same way as your topic; the file or photo itself is never uploaded. The text is kept on your iPhone with the course and is deleted when you delete the course or all your data. It is never included when you share a course.

Sharing a course

If you share a course file, it contains the course plan, the lessons and the written chapters, but never your API key, your name, your progress or your own source material. A file you receive is checked on your iPhone before it is added.

Microphone and speech

For language courses PolyQuest can read words aloud using the voices built into iOS, and can check your speaking practice. Speaking practice uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone only (on-device recognition): the audio is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the feature is simply not offered for languages where on-device recognition is unavailable. You can allow or deny microphone and speech access in iOS Settings at any time.

Talking with Poly (voice conversations)

The Talk tab lets you have a spoken conversation with Poly. You choose how it works, and the default keeps your voice on your iPhone:

  • Standard: your iPhone listens (on-device speech recognition) and speaks (iOS voices). Only the text of the conversation goes to the AI provider you connected (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), exactly like the chat in the Tutor tab. Your voice is not sent anywhere.
  • Natural voice / ElevenLabs voices (optional): the text of Poly's sentences is also sent to the voice service you choose (OpenAI, Google or ElevenLabs, with your own key) to be turned into speech. Your own voice still stays on your iPhone.
  • Live with OpenAI / Live with Gemini (optional, asks your permission first): your voice is streamed while you talk to OpenAI or Google, with your own account and key, so they can answer in real time. PolyQuest does not record or store the audio. The provider's own terms and retention rules apply to what you send them. The call ends by itself after two quiet minutes.

Finished conversations are saved on your iPhone as text only (never as audio) so you can read the review again; you can delete them in the Talk settings or with "Delete all my data". PolyQuest has no servers and no account: nothing is sent to the developer.

Analytics, ads and tracking

PolyQuest contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Donations

Donations are handled on Ko-fi, outside the app, under Ko-fi's own privacy policy. Donating does not unlock anything in the app.

Age

PolyQuest is intended for adults (18+).

Your control

In the Profile tab you can export a readable file of the data the app holds (it never contains your API keys) and delete all your progress, courses and answers. You can remove your API key in the AI settings, and removing the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.
